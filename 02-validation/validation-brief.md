# Validation Brief

> Module 2 · Validation. Frame the riskiest assumption, then build to test it. **This locks your scenario, no switching after M2.**

## Scenario

Vantage Controls (own product)

## Riskiest assumption

_The one belief that, if false, sinks the idea._

The riskiest thing I'm assuming is that action owners can understand what they need to do, what evidence is required, and move an action towards completion without needing additional clarification outside the tool.

## Hypothesis

> We believe **Structure control actions around a clear Finding, Recommendation, and Required Evidence.** will cause **Action Owners understand and complete their assigned actions without additional clarification.** for **Action Owners responsible for resolving control or audit findings.**. We'll know we're right when **Users can correctly identify what they need to do and what evidence they need to provide without external guidance.**.

## Risk type

- [ ] Value (do they want it?)
- [x] Usability (can they use it?)
- [ ] Feasibility (can we build it?)
- [ ] Viability (should we?)

## Kill switch

_The result that would make you stop or pivot._

If the prototype shows that Action Owners still need additional clarification to understand what they need to do or what evidence they need to provide, I stop or pivot.

## What the prototype must test

A user should be able to open an assigned control action and, without additional explanation:

1. Identify the **Finding** — what was identified.
2. Identify the **Recommendation** — what they need to do to resolve it.
3. Identify the **Required Evidence** — what they need to provide to demonstrate completion.
4. Understand how to move the action forward once the required work and evidence are complete.

If the user needs external clarification to complete these steps, the kill switch is triggered.


## The three ingredients

### Real data / domain context

This is a Bring Your Own scenario, so no Product School Context Data Pack is available. The prototype will therefore use realistic, anonymised control-action content based on the original workflow rather than invented performance metrics.

Domain information represented in the prototype includes:

- Finding
- Recommendation
- Required Evidence
- Action Owner
- Reviewer
- Due date and overdue status
- Severity
- Control Area
- Action progress / review status

### User voice

No verbatim user-research quotes are available for this Bring Your Own scenario.

Rather than fabricate quotes, the prototype will test the core usability assumption directly during the Show & Swap: whether a user can understand and act on a control action without additional explanation.
