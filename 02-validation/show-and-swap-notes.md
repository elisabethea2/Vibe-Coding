# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

Vantage Controls validation prototype: https://vantage-controls-validation.lovable.app/. I showed the Action Owner flow for understanding an assigned control action, providing the required evidence, and moving it forward for review.

## What my partner understood (in their words)

They understood that the prototype was testing whether an Action Owner could figure out what needed to be done and what evidence was required without having to ask someone for help.

## Where they got confused / pushed back

They initially weren't sure what “Request clarification” would do or who the clarification would be sent to. They also expected the evidence requirements to be the main guide for completing the action, rather than needing additional instructions elsewhere.

## What I'll change as a result

Make the purpose of “Request clarification” slightly clearer while keeping it secondary to the main action flow, so users know when to use it without making clarification feel like the expected path.
