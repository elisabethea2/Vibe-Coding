## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** Josh understood it as a tool for a services team to track and manage actions that need attention.
- **What confused them:** There were a lot of different details and it wasn't immediately clear how they were grouped into the main themes or what information mattered most.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** That giving users a clear view of the actions that need attention, with the relevant details in one place, would make it easier to prioritise and manage their work.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** The core idea of tracking actions that need attention came through, but the control/audit context was not immediately clear. The amount of information also made it harder to understand the main themes and hierarchy at first glance.
