Module 2 · The frame

Problem & hypothesis

01
The business problem

Replace the legacy system. Preserve the workflow.

A legacy internal application for control and audit actions needs to be decommissioned, but the business process it supports must continue.

The challenge is to preserve the essential workflow in a simpler, purpose-built product that helps teams create, track, document evidence for, review and close control actions.

The hypothesis in practice

Complete assigned actions without additional clarification.

For Action Owners responsible for resolving control or audit findings, we tested whether presenting the Finding, Recommendation and Required Evidence together would help them understand and complete their assigned actions independently.

The test: can Action Owners correctly identify their next steps and the evidence needed, without asking for clarification?

Module 6 · The move

Data signal → one focused fix

03
01

Signal

Peer testing showed that some Action Owners did not immediately understand why an action could not be sent for review, even though the existing guidance helped once found.

02

Fix

Submission requirements were made more visible directly above Send for Review, with a clear explanation and evidence completion progress.

03

Result

The UI now makes the blocker and next required step visible at the point of action.

This describes the implemented UI change. Post-redeploy user validation is still pending.
The call

Recommendation

04
ITERATE

Keep refining.

Peer feedback praised the clear, intuitive interface and highlighted friction around permissions and moving actions to review. Submission requirements are now more visible at the point of action, and the reported evidence download issue has also been fixed.

The recommendation is to keep refining the product based on user feedback, with a focus on helping Action Owners understand and complete the workflow.
